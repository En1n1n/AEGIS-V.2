TERMS OF SERVICE

Welcome to AEGIS! By accessing our website or using our emergency response application, you agree to follow these Terms of Service. These rules help keep our community feed reliable, safe, and focused on real-time disaster coordination.

Acceptable Use

  • Truthful Reporting: You agree to only submit accurate safety check-ins, hazard reports, and mutual aid requests to prevent panic and misinformation.
  • No Spam or False Alarms: Posting fake emergencies, prank SOS alerts, or unrelated promotional content is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate account suspension.
  • Respectful Mutual Aid: Users offering or requesting food, water, shelter, or rescue assistance must interact honestly and respectfully with neighbors and responders.

User Accounts

  • Accurate Information: You are responsible for keeping your contact details and designated emergency contacts accurate and up to date.
  • Account Security: You are responsible for maintaining the security of your login credentials and any activity submitted under your account.
  • Verified Agency Accounts: Only authorized Barangay officials, Local Government Units (LGUs), and verified response agencies may apply for official broadcast privileges.

Emergency Disclaimers

  • Not a Replacement for 911: AEGIS is a community coordination tool and does not replace official emergency hotlines (such as 911, local fire, police, or medical dispatch). Always contact official responders directly in life-threatening situations.
  • Network Availability: While we provide Low Bandwidth Mode and SMS fallback, message delivery depends on third-party mobile carriers and local network infrastructure during severe disasters.
  • Crowdsourced Data: Hazard pins and road status updates are submitted by community members and may change rapidly before being verified.

Content Moderation

  • Content Removal: We reserve the right to remove unverified, harmful, or misleading posts that disrupt emergency response efforts.
  • Changes to Terms: As AEGIS continues to develop, we may update these Terms of Service and notify users of major changes on our website.
